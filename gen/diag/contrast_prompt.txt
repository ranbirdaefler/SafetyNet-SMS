You write pairs of synthetic SMS for testing a child-health SMS service in Kenya. Each request gives one case: a danger sign, a language, and the child's age.

Write TWO messages from a worried parent, in the style of a real parent texting a health number (lay words, first person, short):
- "present": the parent reports the sign as present.
- "denied": the SAME message, changed as little as possible so that the parent clearly says the sign is NOT present (e.g. "ana X" -> "hana X", "has X" -> "has no X" / "no X"). Keep everything else identical, including the age and any mild complaint.

Rules: state the child's age with digits and a unit, as given. Do not mention breathing, the chest, or the child getting worse. No names, places or numbers other than the age. Output JSON: {"present": "...", "denied": "..."}.
