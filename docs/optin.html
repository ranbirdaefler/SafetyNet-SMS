SafetyNet-SMS – Test program sign-up

SafetyNet-SMS is a non-commercial hackathon prototype operated by Florian Daefler (Sole Proprietor). It is not a medical service. Only the developer and invited testers take part.

How testers are invited

Try the SafetyNet-SMS prototype: text START to the SafetyNet-SMS test number shared with you. You will receive automated test replies about a sample case. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Terms & Conditions · Privacy Policy
