About the service

SafetyNet-SMS is a non-commercial hackathon prototype. It is not a medical service, is not clinically validated, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, go to the nearest health facility or call your local emergency number.

SMS Terms

  • Program: SafetyNet-SMS test messages from Florian Daefler.
  • What you receive: automated replies to messages you send, such as "Take the child to the health facility now. Your health worker has been told.", health-worker checklists, and referral or arrival confirmations.
  • Frequency: message frequency varies; messages are sent only in response to your own messages or a case you are part of.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Help: reply HELP or email floriand@me.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
