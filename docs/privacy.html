Privacy Policy

SafetyNet-SMS, operated by Florian Daefler (Sole Proprietor). Last updated: 1 October 2026.

SafetyNet-SMS is a non-commercial prototype built for the World Bank Small AI for Development Hackathon. It demonstrates an SMS service that routes a message about a sick child to a community health worker and a health facility. During development and the demo, messages are exchanged only with the developer's own test phones and invited testers.

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The text of SMS messages you send to and receive from our numbers.
  • Message timestamps and delivery status.

We do not ask for names, addresses or other identifying information, and testers are asked not to include real patient information.

How we use it

  • To process your message and send the automated replies described in our Terms & Conditions.
  • To test, debug and evaluate the prototype.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Messages are carried by our SMS provider (Twilio) solely to deliver them.

Retention

Message logs are kept only for testing and are deleted after the hackathon judging period ends.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. To request deletion of your data, contact the email below.
